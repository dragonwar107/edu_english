7 DIGITAL RISKS

Toasted Skin

2

Obesity

3

Sleep Cycle

4

Hearing Loss

Damage from loud headphones (>85dB).

5

Finger Joints

Stress from rapid, repetitive phone use.

6

Postural Pain

Neck and spine strain from slumped sitting.

7

RSI Injury

7 SOLUTIONS

A. Display Filters

Use blue light filters especially at bedtime.

B. Input Methods

Use voice recognition to reduce finger strain.

C. Spine Care

Sit straight, monitor and keyboard at eye level.

D. Active Lifestyle

Exercise regularly to prevent diseases.

E. Body Support

Use ergonomic gear or protective braces.

F. Air Flow

Use tables or desks to keep laptop vents clear.

G. Volume Check

Cap volume. Ringing ears is a danger sign.
