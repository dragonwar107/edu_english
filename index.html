7 SOLUTIONS

A. Display

Use filters and take regular breaks.

B. Input

Use voice-to-text to reduce strain.

C. Posture

Sit straight, monitor at eye level.

D. Exercise

Stay active to prevent obesity.

E. Ergonomics

Use supports for affected parts.

F. Laptop

Always use a desk or a 'lap desk'.

G. Volume
