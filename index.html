7 DIGITAL RISKS

1

Toasted Skin

Heat damage from balancing laptops on bare skin.

2

Inactivity

Obesity and waistline growth due to screen time.

3

Sleep Cycle

Blue light suppresses melatonin, ruining sleep.

4

Hearing Loss

Permanent ear damage from loud music (>85dB).

5

Finger Joints

Stress from rapid and repetitive phone messaging.

6

Postural Pain

Slumped positions strain muscles and spine joints.

7

RSI Injury

Wrist and joint damage from intense mouse usage.

FLIP FOR SOLUTIONS ➔

7 BEST PRACTICES

A. Display Filters

Adjust settings and use 'blue light' filters at bedtime.

B. Input Methods

Use voice recognition to resolve messaging stress.

C. Spine Care

Sit straight with monitor and keyboard at eye level.

D. Active Lifestyle

Take regular exercise to prevent cardio-vascular disease.

E. Body Support

Use ergonomic mice, keyboards, or special braces.

F. Air Flow

Use tables or lap desks to keep laptop vents clear.

G. Volume Check

Use automatic limits. Ringing in ears is a danger sign.
