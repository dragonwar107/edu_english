Digital Risks

01

Toasted Skin

02

Obesity

Inactivity from screen time leads to weight gain.

03

Sleep Debt

Blue light suppresses melatonin production.

04

Ear Damage

05

Joint Pain

Messaging puts extreme stress on thumb joints.

06

Posture

07

RSI Damage

Smart Habits

A. Use Lap Desks

Keep your laptop on a table or protective surface.

B. Stay Active

Regular exercise prevents cardiovascular diseases.

C. Night Filters

Enable blue light filters 1 hour before sleeping.

D. Volume Cap

Limit audio to 60%. Protect your inner ear cells.

E. Voice Input

Use voice-to-text to give your thumbs a break.

F. Eye Level

Position your screen to keep your back straight.

G. Ergonomics

Use ergonomic mice and take frequent breaks.
